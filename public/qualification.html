Course Qualifications & Eligibility Criteria

Requirements for Certificate, Diploma, and Master Level fine arts accreditations.

1. Junior Certificate Course (1 Year)

Eligibility: Minimum age 8 years. Open to beginners with basic interest in drawing, classical dance, or vocal music.

Evaluation: Practical skill test & simple theory assessment at course end.

2. Senior Diploma Program (3 Years)

Eligibility: Must have completed 10th standard (High School) or possess Junior Certificate in equivalent discipline.

Evaluation: Bi-monthly practical exams and mandatory exhibition presentation.

3. Master Fine Arts Degree (2 Years)

Eligibility: Graduate degree (BFA / BA in Visual Arts/Music) or 3-year Senior Diploma from recognized institution.

Evaluation: Final portfolio thesis display, viva, and solo recital/exhibition.
