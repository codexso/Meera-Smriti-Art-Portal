Building skill, patience and imagination — one drawing at a time.

At Meera Smriti Sishu Ankan Siksha Kendra, we believe art education does more than teach children to draw. It teaches them to observe carefully, think independently, and express themselves with confidence.

Our curriculum is structured by age group, moving students from basic shapes and colour theory through to composition, perspective and personal style, guided by trained art instructors in small batches.

Age-Wise Levels

Separate curricula for ages 4–7, 8–12 and 13+.

Small Batches

Individual attention with limited seats per class.

Certified Progress

Level certificates and annual exhibition of student work.

Trained Faculty

Experienced instructors patient with young learners.
