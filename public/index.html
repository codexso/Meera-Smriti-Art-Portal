Building skill, patience and imagination — one drawing at a time.

At Meera Smriti Sishu Ankan Siksha Kendra, we believe art education does more than teach children to draw. It teaches them to observe carefully, think independently, and express themselves with confidence.

Our curriculum is structured by age group, moving students from basic shapes and colour theory through to composition, perspective and personal style, guided by trained art instructors in small batches.

Age-Wise Levels

Separate curricula for ages 4–7, 8–12 and 13+.

Small Batches

Individual attention with limited seats per class.

Certified Progress

Level certificates and annual exhibition of student work.

Trained Faculty

Experienced instructors patient with young learners.

Plan Your Visit

Timings and fees, tailored to your child

We don't run one fixed timetable. Weekday and weekend batch slots, class frequency, and fees are discussed directly with each family based on the child's age group, chosen programme, and seat availability at the time of enquiry — over a call or WhatsApp, not a price list.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is a free trial class available?

Yes. Every new student can attend one trial class before enrolling, so both the child and parents can see our teaching style firsthand.

What materials does my child need to bring?

A basic art kit list is shared at admission. Foundation-level students can use materials available at the centre for their first few classes.

Can students switch batches or levels later?

Yes, students are reassessed periodically and can move up a level or switch timing slots, subject to seat availability.

How do I get in touch quickly?

The fastest way is WhatsApp using the button at the top or bottom-right of this page. You can also fill the enquiry form and we'll call you back.
