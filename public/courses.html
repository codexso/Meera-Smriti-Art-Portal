Candidate Skill Interaction Process

Why we schedule individual interaction before fixing monthly tuition

Average Range: ₹300 – ₹450 / month
STEP 01
Skill Assessment

Our senior teacher (Srimati Tanisha Pal / Director) evaluates the student's stroke confidence and age level.

STEP 02
Module Allocation

Student is placed into Junior Foundation, Intermediate Landscape, or Senior Diploma track.

STEP 03
Fee Finalization

Monthly tuition is finalized based on module frequency and batch choice (Avg ~₹370/mo).
