Fine Art Course Structure

Structured training levels designed for young beginners to advanced diploma candidates preparing for board examinations.

Under 9 Years

Junior Ankan Stage

Foundation level focused on basic shapes, motor skill development, and color identification.

  • Crayon & Pastel Shading Technique
  • Object Drawing & Geometric Shapes
  • Parent-Assisted Progress Tracking
  • Annual Internal Art Display Entry
Monthly Tuition: ~ ₹350 - ₹370

Ages 9 to 14 Years

Intermediate Art Stage

Transition into watercolor, composition building, and realistic nature studies.

  • Transparent Watercolor Blending
  • Landscape & Perspective Drawing
  • Still Life & Pencil Sketching
  • Annual Exhibition Canvas Selection
Monthly Tuition: ~ ₹370 / month

Senior Fine Arts Diploma
