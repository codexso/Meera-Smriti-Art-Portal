Director's Message

"Art is not merely a hobby; it is a discipline that shapes character, builds focus, and connects the youth to our cultural roots. At Meera Smriti Sishu Ankan Siksha Kendra, our objective is to provide a structured academic environment where students can master the fundamentals of visual arts."

Through rigorous practice, formal exhibitions, and state-level competitions, we prepare our students to excel professionally and culturally. Our curriculum spans across basic sketching to advanced oil painting and diploma-level preparation.

Academic Programs

Structured syllabus designed to take students from foundational techniques to advanced diploma proficiency.

Foundation Course

Primary education focusing on pencil grip, basic shapes, color recognition, and pastel shading fundamentals. Suitable for ages 4 to 8.

View Syllabus
Core Wing

Intermediate Studio

Training in watercolors, gouache techniques, still-life observation, and landscape rendering. Structured for ages 9 to 15.

View Syllabus

Diploma Preparation

Advanced training in oil on canvas, acrylics, anatomical drawing, and fine arts diploma exhibition preparation for senior students.
